Wino Crochet

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026
Applies to the Wino Crochet Android and iOS apps.

1. Local-first app

Wino Crochet is a local-first crochet and knitting companion. Patterns, projects, counters, sessions, notes, stash items, and imported PDFs and photos are stored on your device. The app can be used without an account.

2. Optional account

If you create an account, Wino Crochet uses Supabase authentication to process your email address and maintain your sign-in session. An account is used for sign-in, optional cloud sync, and account deletion.

3. Optional cloud sync and file backup

Cloud sync is available while you are signed in. Cloud Asset Backup requires your explicit in-app consent before files or photos are uploaded. You can revoke that consent in the app; this removes cloud backups and leaves local files on your device.

4. Account deletion

You can start account deletion from the account area in Wino Crochet. If you cannot access the app, email ayoub011@live.fr from the email address linked to your Wino Crochet account and ask us to delete it. Do not send your password. We will verify the request and delete the cloud account and its Cloud Asset Backup data before removing the authentication user. Local app data and files on your device are not removed by cloud account deletion; uninstalling the app removes local data subject to your device's behavior.

5. Your content and files

Content you create and PDFs or photos you import stay on your device unless included in cloud copies that you explicitly consent to or the analytics and crash metadata described in section 6. Wino Crochet does not sell your content or share it with advertisers.

6. Product analytics and crash reporting (Firebase)

Wino Crochet uses Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics to measure installs, understand which features are used, and fix crashes. Collection is on by default on first install. An explicit opt-out stored in the app is always honored and switches Firebase Analytics off, including automatic events; native collection flags default off so nothing is sent before your stored preference is applied at startup. Crash reporting is independent of the analytics setting and stays on so crashes can always be diagnosed.

Analytics events are limited to fixed names with no personal content: first app open (counted once per install), cloud account creation, first project creation (counted once), project completion, and pattern saves labeled only by source kind (blank, file, photo, text, website, youtube, or mixed). Crash reports carry only the app version, build number, platform, operating-system version, and a sanitized error category — never the original error message, stack contents, or user data.

What is never sent to analytics or crash reporting: your name, email, pattern or project content, filenames, photos, URLs, tokens, user IDs, absolute paths, or the advertising identifier (the app does not access it).

To turn usage analytics off, open Account → Usage analytics in the app and switch collection off; the choice takes effect immediately and is remembered. If you cannot access the app, email ayoub011@live.fr to request that analytics collection be turned off. Wino Crochet does not use analytics data for advertising.

7. No ads, no ad tracking

The app shows no advertisements and includes no ad-serving SDKs. No Meta, TikTok, or other advertising SDKs are integrated, and the app does not request App Tracking Transparency or access the advertising identifier. Firebase is used only for the analytics and crash reporting described in section 6.

8. YouTube and Discover

YouTube features use official platform services to access public YouTube content. Wino Crochet does not download or copy third-party patterns. Use of YouTube features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Discover links open attributed external sources in your browser or the official embedded player. Wino Crochet does not proxy that traffic.

9. Data retention

Local data is retained on your device until you delete it in the app or uninstall the app. Consented cloud copies are retained until you revoke consent or delete your account, as described in sections 3–4. Analytics events and crash reports are retained by Google under Google's terms.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email ayoub011@live.fr.

11. Policy changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date and publish the revised policy at this address.
