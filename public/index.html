StitchFlow

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 21, 2026
Applies to the StitchFlow Android and iOS apps.

1. Local-first app

StitchFlow is a local-first crochet and knitting companion. Patterns, projects, counters, sessions, notes, stash items, and imported PDFs and photos are stored on your device. The app can be used without an account.

2. Optional account

If you create an account, StitchFlow uses Supabase authentication to process your email address and maintain your sign-in session. An account is used for sign-in, optional cloud sync, and account deletion.

3. Optional cloud sync and file backup

Cloud sync is available while you are signed in. Cloud Asset Backup requires your explicit in-app consent before files or photos are uploaded. You can revoke that consent in the app; this removes cloud backups and leaves local files on your device.

4. Account deletion

You can start account deletion from the account area in StitchFlow. If you cannot access the app, email ayoub011@live.fr from the email address linked to your StitchFlow account and ask us to delete it. Do not send your password. We will verify the request and delete the cloud account and its Cloud Asset Backup data before removing the authentication user. Local app data and files on your device are not removed by cloud account deletion; uninstalling the app removes local data subject to your device's behavior.

5. Your content and files

Content you create and PDFs or photos you import stay on your device unless included in cloud copies that you explicitly consent to. StitchFlow does not sell your content or share it with advertisers.

6. Optional diagnostics

Remote diagnostics are off by default and only start if you enable the in-app consent control. If diagnostics are configured in the app build, limited diagnostic events may be sent to Sentry, such as app version, build number, platform, operating-system version, selected error category, and app feature. The app's reporting adapter is designed to exclude pattern, project, and stash content; titles; notes; file contents; photos; passwords; tokens; user IDs; and absolute file paths. You can turn reporting off through the same in-app control.

7. Advertising and analytics

StitchFlow does not show advertisements and does not use a third-party product analytics SDK. The only remote diagnostics path is the consent-gated reporting described above.

8. YouTube and Discover

YouTube features use official platform services to access public YouTube content. StitchFlow does not download or copy third-party patterns. Use of YouTube features is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service; Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Discover links open attributed external sources in your browser or the official embedded player.

9. Data retention

Local data remains on your device until you remove it in the app or uninstall the app. Consented cloud copies are retained until you revoke consent or delete your account. Diagnostic reports are retained by the crash-reporting provider under its own terms.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or support, email ayoub011@live.fr.

11. Policy changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date and publish the revised policy at this address.
